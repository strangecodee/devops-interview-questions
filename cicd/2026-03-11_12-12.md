 # Improvement Recommendation
Implementing Blue-Green Deployment strategy for zero-downtime releases using GitLab CI/CD.

# Detailed Explanation
Blue-Green Deployment is an incremental release method that eliminates downtime during application updates by maintaining two identical production environments, named Blue and Green, and gradually shifting traffic between them. During release, the traffic is directed to the new Green environment while the old Blue environment runs the previous version. Once the release is confirmed to be stable, traffic is switched back to the Green environment, and the old Blue environment can be taken offline for updates or decommissioned. This approach ensures minimal downtime and improved application stability during releases.

# Benefits
- Minimizes downtime during releases, improving overall system availability.
- Reduces the risk of failure during deployments as any issues can be easily and quickly rolled back.
- Improves overall deployment efficiency and reliability.
