 # Improvement Recommendation
Adopting GitOps practices for your enterprise CI/CD pipeline.

# Detailed Explanation
GitOps is a modern DevOps practice that utilizes Git as a single source of truth for managing infrastructure and applications. By embracing GitOps, your team can benefit from versioning, rollbacks, and collaboration. This approach enables continuous delivery by automating the release process and enforcing policy checks.

# Benefits
- Enables version control for infrastructure and applications, making it easier to manage and rollback changes.
- Promotes collaboration among teams through a single source of truth for deployments and configuration.
- Automates the release process, ensuring consistent and reliable deployments through policy checks and approvals.
