 # Kubernetes Deployment
This Kubernetes concept refers to the declaration and orchestration of a set of containers, their desired state, and scaling as needed.

# Explanation
A Kubernetes Deployment creates and manages the lifecycle of containerized applications along with their associated resources. It ensures availability, scalability, and rolling updates or rollbacks of your applications.

# Best Practices
- Use labels for easier management and organization of deployments.
- Use rolling updates to minimize downtime and ensure graceful transitions between container versions.
- Use liveness and readiness probes to monitor the health and availability of your containers.

# YAML Example
```yaml
apiVersion: apps/v1
kind: Deployment
metadata:
  name: my-app-deployment
  labels:
    app: my-app
spec:
  replicas: 3
  selector:
    matchLabels:
      app: my-app
  template:
    metadata:
      labels:
        app: my-app
    spec:
      containers:
      - name: my-container
        image: my-container-image:latest
        ports:
        - containerPort: 80
        livenessProbe:
          httpGet:
            path: /healthz
            port: 80
        readinessProbe:
          httpGet:
            path: /ready
            port: 80
```
