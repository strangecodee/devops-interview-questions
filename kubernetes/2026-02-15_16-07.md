 # Kubernetes Concept: Deployment
A Kubernetes Deployment creates and manages a desired state for a group of replicas, ensuring availability and scalability.

# Explanation
Deployments provide declarative updates for Pods and automatically create and manage ReplicaSets for availability and scaling. They continuously rollout new versions of your application and rollback if needed, ensuring zero downtime for your users.

# Best Practices
- Use `rolling-update` strategy to minimize downtime during updates.
- Use `readinessProbes` and `livenessProbes` to ensure your application is healthy and ready to serve traffic.

# YAML Example
```yaml
apiVersion: apps/v1
kind: Deployment
metadata:
  name: atlas-sre
spec:
  replicas: 3
  selector:
    matchLabels:
      app: atlas
      team: sre
  template:
    metadata:
      labels:
        app: atlas
        team: sre
    spec:
      containers:
      - name: atlas
        image: myregistry/atlas:v1.0.0
        ports:
        - containerPort: 80
          name: http
          protocol: TCP
        readinessProbes:
        - exec:
            command: ["curl", "--fail", "/healthz"]
            intervalSeconds: 5
            timeoutSeconds: 1
      livenessProbes:
      - exec:
          command: ["curl", "--fail", "/healthz"]
          intervalSeconds: 30
          timeoutSeconds: 1
```
