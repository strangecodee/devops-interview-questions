 # Kubernetes Concept: Deployment
A Kubernetes Deployment manages the rollout and scaling of containerized applications. It creates and updates Pods in a replicated set, ensuring availability and resilience.

# Explanation
Deployments provide declarative updates for Pods, allowing for seamless rolling updates and rollbacks. They also support auto-scaling based on CPU usage or custom metrics.

# Best Practices
- Use labels for categorizing and selectively managing Deployments.
- Implement a rolling update strategy with a controlled update percentage.
- Use a liveness and readiness probe for your application to ensure proper functioning.

# YAML Example
```yaml
apiVersion: apps/v1
kind: Deployment
metadata:
  name: quantum-deployment
  labels:
    app: quantum
spec:
  replicas: 3
  selector:
    matchLabels:
      app: quantum
  template:
    metadata:
      labels:
        app: quantum
    spec:
      containers:
      - name: quantum
        image: myregistry/quantum:v1
        ports:
        - containerPort: 80
        livenessProbe:
          httpGet:
            path: /healthz
            port: 80
        readinessProbe:
          httpGet:
            path: /ready
            port: 80
```
