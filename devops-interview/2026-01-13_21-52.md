 # Interview Question
Can you explain your experience with Chaos Engineering and how it relates to our quantum computing infrastructure?

# Answer
Chaos Engineering is a disciplined practice of intentionally injecting failure into a system to improve its resilience and availability. It is particularly relevant to our quantum computing infrastructure due to the unique nature of quantum systems and their susceptibility to errors. By simulating potential faults and testing our response to them, we can proactively mitigate risks and improve overall system reliability.

# Key Technical Points
- Chaos Engineering helps identify and remediate potential single points of failure in complex systems.
- It involves deliberately introducing faults in a controlled manner to observe system behavior and response.
- Chaos Engineering can be applied to quantum computing infrastructure by simulating errors in quantum gates and circuits.
