# Real-World Example
I once led a project to implement a monitoring solution for a large financial services organization. We used Prometheus and Grafana to monitor containerized microservices throughout the organization. Ansible was our configuration management tool of choice for automating the deployment and configuration of monitoring agents across multiple teams. We created a matrixed architecture for monitoring, allowing teams to view their specific services while also providing an overall view of the entire system. By implementing this solution, we significantly improved visibility and response time for critical issues.
