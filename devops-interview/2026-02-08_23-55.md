 # Interview Question
Can you explain the concept of on-call rotations in Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) and how they differ from traditional IT operations?

# Answer
On-call rotations are a practice in Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) where engineers are assigned to be responsible for responding to production incidents outside of normal business hours. This approach aims to minimize the impact of incidents on end-users by ensuring that an experienced engineer is always available. The concept differs from traditional IT operations where incident response is usually handled by a dedicated team or external service provider.

# Key Technical Points
- On-call rotations distribute the responsibility of handling production incidents among team members, providing a more balanced workload and reducing the likelihood of burnout.
- SRE teams use on-call schedules to ensure that there's always sufficient coverage for critical systems and applications.
- During their on-call shift, engineers are expected to monitor systems, respond to alerts, and collaborate with other teams to resolve incidents.

# Real-World Example
```yaml
on_call_schedule:
  - engineer1:
      day: monday, wednesday, friday
      start_time: 16:00
      end_time: 08:00
  - engineer2:
      day: tuesday, thursday, saturday
      start_time: 08:00
      end_time: 16:00
  - engineer3:
      day: sunday, monday, wednesday, friday
      start_time: 08:00
      end_time: 16:00
```

In this example, three engineers are scheduled for on-call duties, each responsible for specific days and shifts. This configuration ensures that there is always someone available to respond to incidents during non-business hours.
