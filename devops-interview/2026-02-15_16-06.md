 # Interview Question
Can you explain the concept of on-call rotations in the context of Site Reliability Engineering (SRE)?

# Answer
On-call rotations are a key practice in Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) where team members take turns being available for incident response and problem resolution outside of normal business hours. This ensures that there is always someone responsible for maintaining the health and availability of production systems.

# Key Technical Points
- Rotations typically last for a specific duration, such as a week, and team members are given sufficient notice to plan their availability.
- An effective on-call rotation system allows for fair distribution of responsibilities and minimizes the impact on sleep patterns and work-life balance.
- Tools like PagerDuty and OpsGenie can be used to manage on-call rotations, notifications, and escalation processes.

# Real-World Example
Using PagerDuty, we can set up on-call rotations for our team by defining schedules for team members and setting up escalation policies to ensure that incidents are always responded to in a timely and effective manner.

```yaml
on_call_schedule:
  - user: "user1"
    schedule:
      start_time: "2023-03-01T00:00:00Z"
      end_time: "2023-03-07T23:59:59Z"
  - user: "user2"
    schedule:
      start_time: "2023-03-08T00:00:00Z"
      end_time: "2023-03-14T23:59:59Z"
  # ...
```
