 # Interview Question
Can you explain the concept of on-call rotation and its importance in Site Reliability Engineering (SRE)?

# Answer
On-call rotation is a practice in Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) where team members take turns being responsible for incident response and handling production issues. This approach ensures that team members are always available to address critical production incidents and maintain system reliability. The importance of on-call rotation lies in ensuring that the team is adequately staffed to handle incidents, reducing the workload on individual team members, and providing opportunities for knowledge transfer and skill development.

# Key Technical Points
- Ensures adequate coverage for handling production incidents
- Reduces workload on individual team members
- Facilitates knowledge transfer and skill development

# Real-World Example
Let's consider a hypothetical team at a financial services company using Google's SRE practices. The team follows a weekly on-call rotation, where each team member is on-call for one week every three weeks. During their on-call week, they are responsible for handling any incidents that may arise. They can be reached via a dedicated communication channel and are expected to respond to incidents within a specified time. The team uses tools like PagerDuty for managing and automating on-call rotations and incident response. This approach helps ensure that they always have someone available to handle production issues and maintain system reliability.
