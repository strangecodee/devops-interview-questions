 # Interview Question
Can you explain the concept of on-call rotation in the context of Site Reliability Engineering (SRE)?

# Answer
On-call rotation is a practice in Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) where team members take turns being responsible for the monitoring and maintenance of production systems during specified timeframes. This approach ensures that there is always a knowledgeable and experienced engineer available to handle incidents and prevent potential service disruptions. By distributing the on-call responsibilities evenly among team members, the workload is balanced, and team members can plan their schedules accordingly.

# Key Technical Points
- Ensures 24/7 coverage for monitoring and incident response
- Distributing the on-call workload among team members
- Promotes a culture of ownership and accountability

# Real-World Example
In a SRE team using Google's SRE Book on-call procedures, team members might be on-call for one week every three months. During their on-call period, they are expected to be reachable for incident responses. They can use tools like PagerDuty or OpsGenie to manage their on-call responsibilities and ensure a smooth on-call rotation.
